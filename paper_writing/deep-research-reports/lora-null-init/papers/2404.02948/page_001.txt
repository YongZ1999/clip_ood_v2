1 Introduction
Fine-tuning large language models (LLMs) is a highly effective technique for boosting their capabili-
ties in various tasks [1] (2) [3] 4], ensuring models to follow instructions [5] {6|{7], and instilling models
with desirable behaviors while eliminating undesirable ones [8]|9]. However, the fine-tuning process
for very large models is accompanied by prohibitive costs. For example, regular 16-bit fine-tuning
of a LLaMA 65B parameter model requires over 780 GB of GPU memory [10], and the VRAM
consumption for training GPT-3 175B reaches 1.2TB [11]. Consequently, various parameter-efficient
fine-tuning (PEFT) methods have been proposed to reduce the number of parameters and
memory usage required for fine-tuning. Due to the ability to maintain the performance of full fine-
tuning without adding additional inference latency, Low-Rank Adaptation (LoORA) has emerged
as a popular PEFT method.
